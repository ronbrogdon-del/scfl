Adjacent Research

SCFL / UCMS — Related measurement and systems work

This page collects research programs, methods, and domains that sit adjacent to SCFL/UCMS. They are listed for orientation and comparison. Inclusion does not imply endorsement, equivalence, or joint work.

Structural Health Monitoring

Fiber-optic, accelerometer, and multi-sensor instrumentation of bridges, buildings, and civil infrastructure. Relevant for INFRA-coded engagements.

  • Princeton Streicker Bridge SHM
  • Caltech Community Seismic Network
  • UCLA Factor Building
  • USGS National Strong-Motion Project / CESMD

Grid and Power-System Measurement

Synchrophasor, microgrid, and control-room research. Relevant for GRID-coded engagements.

  • Texas A&M Smart Grid Center / RELLIS
  • UC San Diego DERConnect / campus microgrid
  • National Laboratory of the Rockies (ARIES / ESIF)
  • Idaho National Laboratory CITRC

Building and Campus Living Labs

Instrumented buildings and multi-energy campuses used for energy, HVAC, and building-to-grid studies.

  • LBNL FLEXLAB
  • University of Michigan and NC State living labs
  • Texas A&M Smart and Connected Homes Testbed

Coherence and Relational Measurement

Other lines of work that address coherence, relational structure, or system integrity as measurable properties. These are neighboring classes, not the same method.

  • Early-warning signal (EWS) literature (distinguished from recoverability reading)
  • Network and interdependence analysis in critical infrastructure
  • Organizational and institutional resilience research

Notes

This list is partial and will be updated as additional adjacent work is identified. It is not a claim of collaboration or of methodological equivalence.
