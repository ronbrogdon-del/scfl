The Process

Four Steps. One Structural Reading.

The challenge runs through UCMS's Structural Intake Engine — a four-stage process that takes a business question in plain language and returns a structural reading without requiring access to your internal systems.

1

Register

A qualifying officer or board member submits the registration. This is a structural requirement, not a credential check — the reading is only actionable if it lands with someone who has the authority to act on it.

2

Submit One Question

A single business question, in plain language, submitted through the portal. The question doesn't need to use structural terminology. The Structural Intake Engine translates it — categorizing it, identifying the relevant seam types, detecting adjacent structural plates, and assessing what telemetry exists or can be specified.

3

Receive the Data Gap Report

The first output is a structural map: what SCFL can reach, what requires specification, what is genuinely absent, and what adjacent structural zones were identified that the question didn't name. Missing data is not a gap — it is a reading. The absence tells you something about how that seam has been managed.

4

The Gate — Three Possible Outcomes

The Structural Intake Engine evaluates the signal. One of three outcomes follows. Each outcome is a real answer — including the ones that stop the engagement.

The Gate

Three Outcomes. All of Them Honest.

The engagement proceeds only when the structural signal is present. The value of a confirmed reading depends entirely on the credibility of the readings that were not confirmed.

Signal Found

The engagement continues

A structural reading is present and actionable. The engagement advances to a Public-Record Structural Assessment — a map of your structural topology as visible in publicly observable data. From there, the path to full MaaS telemetry is open.

Weak Signal

Adjacent plates present

The primary question doesn't produce a clean structural reading — but adjacent structural zones were identified. A Limited Probe Offer is made: bounded strictly to those adjacent plates, public-record only, delivered as a single structured note within a fixed time window. Scope, exclusions, and delivery format are set in writing before any work begins. This is a structured no to the original question — not a conditional yes to something broader.

No Signal

The engagement stops

No structural reading is produced within available telemetry and public-record context. The engagement ends. A sparse reading is not a partial yes — it is a no. This protects the integrity of every reading that does continue.

Who Can Register

Two Conditions. Both Structural.

The registration gate is not a credential filter. It is a structural requirement. A reading is only valuable if it lands with someone positioned to act on it — and if the organization has a measurable state space for SCFL to reach.

01

Decision Altitude

The registrant must be an officer with enterprise risk, capital, or operational authority — or a board member. A structural finding delivered below that altitude has no structural value. It has nowhere to land. A subordinate may draft the question; the qualifying registrant submits it.

02

Measurable State Space

The organization must have a structural condition that SCFL can reach — seams, load signatures, or coherence drift that exists in the telemetry or can be specified. Organizational size is not a condition. A going concern of any size can possess a measurable state space.

On organizational size. Size is a correlate of yield density — larger organizations tend to produce higher seam counts and richer intersection signatures — but it is not a gate. The Structural Intake Engine's Data Availability Assessment performs the actual test: what SCFL can reach, what requires specification, and what is genuinely absent. That assessment determines what a given engagement can produce. Size does not.

Why It Works as a Proof of Concept

Low Risk. High Signal.

The challenge is designed to be the lowest-commitment structural reading that could produce a meaningful result.

  • ✓ No data leaves your environment The Structural Intake Engine operates on the structure implied by the question and your registration profile. It does not request files, system access, or internal data at any stage of the challenge.
  • ✓ No implementation required SCFL does not require you to install anything, integrate a system, or run a pilot program before a structural reading is produced.
  • ✓ One question, one point in time The challenge produces a single structural slice at a single moment. It is not a commitment to ongoing measurement. If the signal is present, a bounded next-steps map is offered — accepted or declined separately.
  • ✓ Missing data is a signal, not a problem If your organization has no telemetry in a given seam domain, that absence is itself a structural reading. You will know something about how that seam has been managed — or hasn't been.
