Enron Reconstruction

A retrospective reading of the Enron collapse through the SCFL lens: variance collapse, cohort-level propagation, and the rupture horizon that was visible in the email corpus months before external indicators moved.

This reconstruction sits alongside SVB, ERCOT, and the other public structural integrity scans. The core observation is that organizational rupture was preceded by a measurable decay regime — persistent negative drift and variance collapse — while conventional performance and compliance signals remained stationary.
