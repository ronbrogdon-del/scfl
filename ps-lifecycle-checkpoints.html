Problem Space 5 of 7 · Lifecycle checkpoints

Did we start where we think? Did the repair actually restore structure?

Two moments anchor a system's record. At handover, the as-built structural state is locked as a versioned baseline, so later readings are compared to what was built, not to design intent. After a repair, the instrument checks whether structural state was actually restored, or only the output.

You'll recognize this if the as-built record is still the design drawings, or a repair closed the work order but the system never quite behaved the same.

What you receive

A locked as-built baseline, and a before-and-after structural reading around each repair.

When to use it

At commissioning or acquisition, and after any significant repair or intervention.

What it starts from

Handover and maintenance records, and dated records before and after the work.
