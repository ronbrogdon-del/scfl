Interior Coherence Family

Souljourner

The interior-coherence family. Distinct from the SCFL systems measurement program. Souljourner addresses biological and interior plates — the relationships that hold a person together under stress.

Distinction: Souljourner is not the identity of the SCFL systems measurement program. SCFL reads non-biological systems (grid, hospitals, firms, cascades). Souljourner is the declared territory for interior and biological plates. No interior operator is Locked. The product funnel (wearables, DTx, XR, games) remains separate from the systems work.

The video above is the introduction to that territory: mind, heart, and the structures that hold them in place — read as coherence, not as performance or pathology.
