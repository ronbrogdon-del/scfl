LAYER
Measurement only. No SCFL operators are applied. This record defines how two
independently maintained series are brought into relation and how the
displacement between them is displayed.

SERIES A: AIS-derived transits
  Source:   IMF PortWatch, Daily_Chokepoints_Data (ArcGIS FeatureServer),
            portid = 'chokepoint2' (Panama Canal)
  Quantity: n_total, summed over each calendar month
  Notes:    PortWatch values may be revised after first publication. The page
            uses the values PortWatch serves at the time of viewing and shows
            the date the data runs through. A month is used only if every
            calendar day of that month is present in Series A.

SERIES B: Official transits
  Source:   Panama Canal Authority (ACP), Advisory to Shipping,
            "Monthly Canal Operations Summary"
  Quantity: "Oceangoing Transits", Total, for the month covered
  Notes:    Entered by hand from the published advisory. Each entry records
            the advisory number and the PDF link. Figures are entered as
            published and are not adjusted.

KNOWN DEFINITIONAL DIFFERENCE
ACP counts oceangoing transits by its own records and beam classes. PortWatch
counts vessels from AIS signals by ship type. No category mapping is applied
to either series. The difference in definitions is part of what the seam
displays and is not corrected for.

DISPLACEMENT (computed per month, both series as defined above)
  Gap           = A - B                (vessels)
  Relative gap  = (A - B) / B          (shown as a percentage)

RULES
  1. Months: January 2025 onward, plus any later month for which both series
     are available. Months missing either series are not shown and are not
     interpolated.
  2. No smoothing, no thresholds, no state labels, and no colour bands are
     applied to the seam. It is displayed as recorded.
  3. Every month that meets rule 1 is shown. No month is excluded after the
     data are examined.
  4. Any change to this rule is made by a new, dated revision of this file,
     applies only to months entered after the revision, and is noted on the
     page.

EXTERNAL VERIFICATION

the figure. Each Series A figure must be reproducible from the PortWatch
query shown in the page's Method section.
