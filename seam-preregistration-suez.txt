LAYER
Measurement only. No SCFL operators are applied. This record defines how two
independently maintained series are brought into relation and how the
displacement between them is displayed. It follows the same rules as the
Panama Canal seam (seam-preregistration-panama.txt).

SERIES A: AIS-derived transits
  Source:   IMF PortWatch, Daily_Chokepoints_Data (ArcGIS FeatureServer),
            portid = 'chokepoint1' (Suez Canal)
  Quantity: n_total, summed over each calendar month
  Notes:    PortWatch values may be revised after first publication. The page
            uses the values PortWatch serves at the time of viewing. A month
            is used only if every calendar day of that month is present.

SERIES B: Official transits
  Source:   Suez Canal Authority (SCA), publications on suezcanal.gov.eg only.
            Primary: the SCA annual navigation report, monthly table
            "NO ( Vessel )" (for 2025: Table (2), "Monthly NO & Net Ton").
            Also admissible: an SCA statement on suezcanal.gov.eg that gives
            the vessel count for one complete calendar month.
  Quantity: number of vessels for the month, as published
  Notes:    Entered by hand. Each entry records the document and its link.
            Figures reported only by third parties (news outlets, brokers)
            are not used. If an annual report and an earlier SCA statement
            differ for the same month, the annual report figure is used and
            the difference is noted.

KNOWN DEFINITIONAL DIFFERENCE
SCA counts vessels by its own transit records (both directions, all ship
types it records). PortWatch counts vessels from AIS signals by ship type. No
category mapping is applied. The difference in definitions is part of what
the seam displays and is not corrected for.

DISPLACEMENT (computed per month)
  Gap           = A - B                (vessels)
  Relative gap  = (A - B) / B          (shown as a percentage)

RULES
  1. Months: January 2025 onward, for which both series are available.
     Months missing either series are not shown and are not interpolated.
  2. No smoothing, no thresholds, no state labels, and no colour bands.
  3. Every month that meets rule 1 is shown. No month is excluded after the
     data are examined.
  4. Any change to this rule is made by a new, dated revision of this file,
     applies only to months entered after the revision, and is noted on the
     page.

EXTERNAL VERIFICATION
Each Series B figure must be traceable to the SCA document linked beside it.
Each Series A figure must be reproducible from the PortWatch query shown in
the page's Method section.

DISCLOSURE
Before registration, no Series B monthly figure from an SCA document had
been read. News headlines seen while locating sources mentioned Suez monthly
traffic levels in 2026 (e.g. a month above 1,300 ships); these are third-party
reports and are not used. No Series A monthly totals had been computed.
